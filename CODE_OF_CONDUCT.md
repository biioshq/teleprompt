# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to Teleprompt to be a straightforward, unremarkable
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What good looks like

- Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept feedback the same way. Nobody's first patch is their best one.
- Be explicit. "This breaks on Safari because the wake lock is dropped" is
  useful; "this is wrong" is not.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, fix it, and move on.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through [biios.in/contact](https://biios.in/contact).
Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not shared.

Maintainers will respond in a way proportionate to what happened: a private
correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from participation.
Maintainers who do not act in good faith may face the same consequences.
